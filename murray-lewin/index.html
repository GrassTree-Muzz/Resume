Fortescue - Asset Maintenance Specialist

I heard about this role through my brother, who works at Fortescue, and it stood out immediately.

Most of my work sits in maintenance strategy optimisation and the master data underneath it - FLOC hierarchies, BOM validation, material classification and work order coding - because a strategy is only ever as good as the data it runs on.

That has been the practical reality of the past two years: bad actor rankings that only hold up once asset criticality and functional locations are right; changeout analysis that depends on PM01/PM02/PM03 notifications being coded consistently; Weibull and MTBF numbers that fall apart if component history is attached to the wrong FLOC. Most recently I have been building the human-in-the-loop workflows that let engineers review and accept AI-generated material classifications and FLOC BOM validations at scale, rather than one spreadsheet at a time - which is the same master data problem this role is set up to attack.

I have done that work inside a multi-disciplinary squad alongside software engineers, data engineers and reliability engineers, iterating quickly on real maintenance data. Fortescue’s approach of leading through doing, and the chance to build proof-of-concept tools alongside that team rather than hand requirements over, is exactly the environment I am looking for.
