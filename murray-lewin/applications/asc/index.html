Why ASC

ASC's work has a purpose I find genuinely compelling - sustaining the Collins Class fleet today while standing up the submarine build and sustainment program for tomorrow. I would like to bring my asset management and reliability background to that effort.

At Deloitte I work with maintenance data, reliability analysis and engineering applications for resources sector clients: an Asset Health Index that tracks fixed-plant health, a maintenance tactics review that found where reliability was being lost, and an asset management framework built with international stakeholders. The assets are different, but the job of turning maintenance data into a clear, defensible decision is the same one I already do. I am realistic that I am coming from mining rather than maritime defence, and I see this role as a genuine step toward the kind of purposeful engineering work I want to build a career in.
