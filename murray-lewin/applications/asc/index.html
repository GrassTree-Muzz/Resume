Why ASC

ASC's work has a purpose I find genuinely compelling. I have always enjoyed the practical side of engineering - understanding how complex equipment is maintained, making the information useful for the people doing the work, and seeing a better decision turn into a real improvement. The chance to bring that mindset to sustaining the Collins Class fleet, and to the submarine build and sustainment program ahead, is one I would be proud to take on.

The assets are different, but the job of turning maintenance data into a clear, defensible decision is the same one I already do. I am realistic that I am coming from mining rather than maritime defence, and I see this role as a genuine step toward the kind of purposeful engineering work I want to build a career in.

Maintenance System Optimisation (MSO) · Consultant EngineerResources Sector ClientJanuary 2026 - Current

Deloitte delivered a full-stack, cloud-native AI application for the client to modernise and automate maintenance strategy processes across Heavy Mobile Equipment (HME) and Fixed Plant (FXP) assets. The platform replaces fragmented, manual engineering workflows with AI-augmented data review, statistical reliability modelling, and data-driven maintenance optimisation, enabling engineers to shift from reactive to proactive asset management. The solution integrates with Databricks and SAP work order systems to provide end-to-end traceability from raw operational data through to validated maintenance recommendations, delivering projected annual savings of $35 million through reduced unplanned downtime and optimised component replacement cycles.
