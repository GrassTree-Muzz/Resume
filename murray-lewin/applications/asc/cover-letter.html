Dear ASC Recruitment Team,

I am applying for the Asset Engineer role with ASC, based in Henderson.

I currently work as a Consultant Engineer at Deloitte, in an asset management team that works with maintenance data, reliability analysis and engineering applications for resources sector clients. Over the past two years I have supported an Asset Health Index rollout for fixed plant assets, contributed to an asset management framework built with stakeholders across several countries, and worked on a maintenance tactics review that looked at downtime and asset criticality to find where reliability was being lost. Most recently, I have been part of a team building an engineering application that supports maintenance strategy review for mobile equipment and fixed plant - combining Weibull-based reliability analysis, asset health scoring and SAP maintenance data into decision-support tools for engineers.

I understand that the assets I would be working with at ASC - submarines rather than mining plant - are a significant step up in complexity and consequence, and that I am coming to this from a different industry. What I can offer is a genuine, demonstrated ability to do the underlying work: turning maintenance and reliability data into decisions that hold up, working closely with engineering and site stakeholders, and being comfortable with the SAP, Power BI and analytical tools that asset management runs on day to day.

I am drawn to ASC because the work has a clear and serious purpose - sustaining the Collins Class fleet and building toward Australia's future submarine capability - and because Henderson is close to home for me in Cottesloe. I would welcome the opportunity to bring my asset management and reliability background to that work, and to keep developing it in a defence context.
